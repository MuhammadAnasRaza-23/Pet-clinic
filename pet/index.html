What We Offer

Complete Care Under One Roof

From preventive wellness to complex surgeries, our team is equipped to care for dogs, cats, birds, and exotic pets with warmth & excitement!

🩺

General Check-ups

Regular wellness exams to keep your pet healthy and catch any concerns early before they become serious.

💉

Vaccinations

Complete vaccination schedules for puppies, kittens, and adult pets — protecting them against common diseases.

🔬

Diagnostics & Lab

In-house blood work, urinalysis, X-rays, and ultrasound for fast, accurate results without the wait.

🏥

Surgery

Spay, neuter, and soft tissue surgical procedures performed by board-certified surgeons with full anesthesia monitoring.

🦷

Dental Care

Professional cleaning, tooth extractions, and oral health assessments to keep your pet's mouth in great shape.

🚨

Emergency Care

Our emergency line is available 24/7. For life-threatening situations, our on-call vets are always ready.
